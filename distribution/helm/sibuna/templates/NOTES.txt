Sibuna uses a single writer with Recreate upgrades. Back up the PVC before upgrade.
The PVC is retained on uninstall; erase it and the operator-owned Secret explicitly
only when retiring the installation. Do not share this PVC between releases.
Health probes test the listener, not origin reachability or storage durability.
Allow trusted callers and the IP-literal origin in networkPolicy values. Your CNI
must implement NetworkPolicy. Protect /__sibuna/* at any external ingress.
No console, ingress, TLS termination or administrator account is created.
