Sibuna runs as a single writer with Recreate upgrades. Back up the PVC before upgrading.
The PVC is kept on uninstall; erase it and the operator-owned Secret explicitly only
when retiring the installation. Do not share this PVC between releases.
Health probes test the listener, not origin reachability or storage durability.
Allow trusted callers and the IP-literal origin in the networkPolicy values. Your CNI
must enforce NetworkPolicy. Protect /__sibuna/* at any external ingress.
No console, ingress, TLS termination or administrator account is created.
