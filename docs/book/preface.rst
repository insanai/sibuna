Preface
=======

Why put another program in front of an application? Running two copies lets us keep serving while one restarts. Both copies are still vulnerable to an expensive request repeated a thousand times. Sibuna grew out of these two problems. This book asks what a program at the entrance can decide, what each decision costs, and how we can check that it was right.

We begin with a journey that needs no knowledge of Rust, cryptography or configuration. In the design chapters we follow the same decisions into algorithms and code. The measurements and operating procedures come later, once we know what the system is trying to achieve.
